    Sleep hygiene awareness tips

    • Keep a regular sleep and wake time, even on weekends.
    • Stop screens 30–60 minutes before bed — the light keeps your brain awake.
    • Avoid tea, coffee or cola in the late afternoon and evening.
    • Keep your room cool, dark and quiet if you can.
    • Use the bed for sleeping — do homework and scrolling elsewhere.
